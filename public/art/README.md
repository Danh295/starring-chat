# Drop-in hand-drawn art

Put your own PNGs here and the overlay picks them up automatically (reload the browser source). With no files here, the card is drawn with CSS/SVG, so nothing is required.

| File | Used for | Suggested size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `card-frame.png` | Frame/background of every role card (text sits on top of it; keep the middle calm) | 520 x 220 px (or 2x: 1040 x 440), transparent corners |
| `stamp.png` | Small corner stamp on each card | 128 x 128 px, transparent |
| `background.png` | Soft backdrop behind the card area (shown at 35% opacity) | 1200 x 700 px |

Guest Star and Background Extra cards keep their gold / ghost tint underneath your frame.
Only use art you made or have the rights to use.
