setup

Turn your chat into the cast of a sitcom. It takes about two minutes and there is nothing to download.

#your-channel

#add-to-obs

  1. In your scene, select + under Sources and choose Browser (Streamlabs: Browser Source).
  2. Paste your overlay URL. Set Width 1920 and Height 1080, and tick Control audio via OBS so the sting plays through your mix.
  3. Select OK, then type in your own chat. Your first message gets a role card in the bottom left. Delete the source to turn it off.

Cards come one at a time and leave after a few seconds, so they never cover your game for long.

#tell-chat

Paste this into chat once. It has no links, so link filters leave it alone.

#chat-commands

  • !lurkAnyone can become a Background Extra.
  • !nocastAnyone can opt out. Their card is removed and they are never cast again.
  • !creditsYou or a mod roll the end credits (about 45 seconds).
  • !newepisodeYou or a mod start a fresh episode with a clean cast.
  • !uncast @nameYou or a mod remove someone from tonight's cast.

Prefer buttons? Open the control room. It needs your CONTROL_PASSWORD.
