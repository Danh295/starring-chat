Starring: Chat

Turn your chat into the cast of a sitcom. Setup takes about two minutes, and there is nothing to download.

1. Your channel

2. Add it to OBS or Streamlabs

  1. In your scene, click + under Sources and choose Browser (Streamlabs: Browser Source).
  2. Paste your overlay URL. Set Width 1920 and Height 1080, and tick Control audio via OBS so the little sting sound is part of your mix.
  3. Click OK and type in your own chat. Your first message gets a role card in the bottom left. Remove the source any time to turn it off.

Cards appear one at a time and stay for a few seconds, so they never cover your game for long.

3. Tell chat what is happening

Paste this into chat once (it has no links, so link filters leave it alone):

Chat commands

  • !lurk anyone: become a Background Extra.
  • !nocast anyone: opt out. Your card is removed and you are never cast again.
  • !credits you or a mod: roll the end credits (about 45 seconds).
  • !newepisode you or a mod: start a fresh episode with a clean cast.
  • !uncast @name you or a mod: remove someone from tonight's cast.

Prefer buttons? Open the control page (needs your CONTROL_PASSWORD).
